## Engineering Operating Model

1. **INTAKE** — Capture the request, device state, context, and available evidence.
2. **VALIDATION** — Separate evidence quality from confidence.
3. **ANALYSIS** — Identify fault signatures, patterns, anomalies, and likely routing paths.
4. **AUTOMATION** — Standardize repeatable work while retaining approval controls.
5. **OBSERVABILITY** — Use telemetry, logs, dashboards, and before/after validation.
